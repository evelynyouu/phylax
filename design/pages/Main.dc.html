Three steps from a messy inbox to a clear list

  1. 1

    Connect Gmail

    Sign in with Google and grant read-only access. Subwatch only looks at billing emails: receipts, invoices, renewal notices and trial reminders.

  2. 2

    We find what repeats

    Charges are matched to the company that sent them, grouped into subscriptions and bills, and checked for price changes and trials about to convert.

  3. 3

    Keep or cancel

    See what is due and when, then cancel straight from the link in the company's own email. Subwatch warns you if a link points anywhere else.

Your email stays your email

Subwatch is built to read as little as it can and keep even less.

  • Read-only Gmail access

    It can't send, label, archive or delete anything. Disconnect at any time and access is revoked.

  • No email text stored

    Only the merchant, amount, dates and cancel link from each billing email are saved.

  • Look-alike senders ignored

    An email that claims to be Netflix but comes from another domain never reaches your list.

  • Cancel links checked

    A link that leaves the sender's own website shows you the real address before it opens.
